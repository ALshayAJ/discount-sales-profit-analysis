# Discount Impact on Sales and Profit Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the relationship between discount levels, sales, and profit using the Supermart Grocery Sales – Retail Analytics dataset.

The objective is to understand how different discount levels are associated with sales performance and profitability.

## Business Questions

- How are discount levels related to total sales?
- How are discount levels related to total profit?
- Which discount level records the highest total sales and profit?
- Do higher discounts consistently lead to higher profits?
- How does average profit vary across discount levels?

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Charts and Data Visualization
- KPI Analysis

## Dataset

**Source:** [Supermart Grocery Sales – Retail Analytics Dataset on Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mohamedharris/supermart-grocery-sales-retail-analytics-dataset)

**Records Analyzed:** 9,994

## Key Findings

- The 25% discount level recorded the highest total sales at 657.31K.
- The 25% discount level recorded the highest total profit at 164.58K.
- The 25% discount level recorded 438 orders, the highest order count among the discount levels.
- Average profit varied across discount levels, with no consistent decline as discount levels increased.

## Recommendations

- Evaluate discount strategies using both sales and profit metrics.
- Avoid assuming that higher discounts always produce better results.
- Monitor total profit and average profit when comparing discount levels.
- Test different discount strategies before making broad pricing decisions.

## Conclusion

The analysis found that the 25% discount level recorded the highest total sales and profit in this dataset. However, the results do not establish that the discount level itself caused these outcomes.

This project demonstrates the use of Excel to explore business performance, identify patterns, and develop data-driven recommendations.
